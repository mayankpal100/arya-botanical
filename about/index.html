Our Story

Traditional Sourcing, Modern Exports

Arya Botanicals Global was founded with a simple belief: that India's rich botanical heritage deserves world-class export standards. Starting from the herb trading heartland of Gujarat, we built relationships with cultivators, processors, and manufacturers across India.

Over the years, we developed a vertically integrated supply chain — from farm-level sourcing to GMP-certified processing, quality laboratory testing, and export-ready packaging. Today, we serve importers, wholesalers, brands, and industrial buyers in 25+ countries.

Our philosophy is straightforward: reliable sourcing, consistent quality, transparent pricing, and responsive service. No inflated claims — just honest, dependable business partnerships built over years of consistent delivery.

Our Mission

To be the most reliable long-term supply partner for global buyers of Indian botanical products. We aim to deliver consistent quality, transparent pricing, and responsive service with every order — building partnerships that last beyond single transactions.

Our Vision

To position Indian botanicals as the global standard for quality and value. By strengthening farmer networks, investing in modern processing, and maintaining export compliance — we make it effortless for international buyers to source from India.
