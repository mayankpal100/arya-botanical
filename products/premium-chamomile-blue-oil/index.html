Product Overview

Chamomile Blue Oil, derived from the flowers of Matricaria recutita, is a premium essential oil known for its calming and soothing properties. Utilizing steam distillation as its extraction method, this oil captures the essence of chamomile, providing a rich aroma reminiscent of its natural habitat in Bosnia, Spain, and Hungary. The extracted oil is categorized as a middle note, making it a versatile ingredient in various formulations. Our Chamomile Blue Oil is highly sought after in the cosmetic, pharmaceutical, and food industries due to its numerous benefits. It is renowned for its anti-inflammatory, antiseptic, and calming effects, making it a staple in aromatherapy and skincare products. As a B2B supplier, Arya Botanicals ensures that each batch of oil meets stringent quality standards, allowing businesses to create premium products that resonate with their customers. With over 20 years of experience in exporting high-quality Indian botanicals, Arya Botanicals is committed to providing our partners with exceptional products that adhere to international quality certifications. Our sourcing process involves rigorous quality checks and partnerships with reliable farms, ensuring that our Chamomile Blue Oil is not only pure but also ethically sourced. We cater to various packaging needs, making it easy for businesses to incorporate this versatile oil into their offerings.
